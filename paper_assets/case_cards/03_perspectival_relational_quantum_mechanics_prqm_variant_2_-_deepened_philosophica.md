**概述**:
Perspectival Relational Quantum Mechanics (PRQM) proposes a layered reality: fundamental reality consists of objective relational facts, which are indeterminate propensities between interacting systems. These propensities are inherent dispositions for specific outcomes to actualize. The quantum wave function, however, is an agent's probabilistic knowledge of the *potential definite outcomes* they can actualize from these objective relational propensities, from their unique perspective. 'Measurement' is rigorously redefined as a specific physical interaction initiated by a complex 'agent' system, leading to the *actualization* of a definite outcome from the pre-existing relational potentiality *for that specific agent's experience*. The 'collapse' is then strictly an epistemic update of the agent's knowledge. This framework rigorously unites RQM's objective relationalism (of potentialities) with QBism's agent-centric view (of actualized definiteness), providing a consistent mechanism for how objective, indeterminate reality is experienced as definite by individual agents, thereby offering a solution to the quantum measurement problem. Crucially, PRQM predicts specific deviations from standard interpretations in multi-agent scenarios like Wigner's Friend, where the definiteness of outcomes is delayed until an agent's interaction, and potentially measurable signatures distinguishing agentic actualization from mere decoherence.
