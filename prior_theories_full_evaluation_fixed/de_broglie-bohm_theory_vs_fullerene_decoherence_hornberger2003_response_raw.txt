A crucial aspect of dBBT, as stated in its 'Relation to SQM' and 'Formalism' sections, is that its statistical predictions are identical to those of SQM, provided the 'quantum equilibrium hypothesis' ($\rho = |\Psi|^2$) holds for the initial particle distribution. This experiment, measuring fringe visibility, is a statistical experiment and does not aim to probe quantum non-equilibrium. Therefore, we assume quantum equilibrium, and dBBT's statistical prediction for fringe visibility will be identical to SQM's.\n\n1.  **De Broglie-Bohm's View of Decoherence:** In dBBT, the wave function $\Psi$ always evolves unitarily according to the Schrödinger equation: $i\hbar(\partial\Psi/\partial t) = H\Psi$. There is no wave function collapse. When a system (like a C70 fullerene) interacts with its environment (e.g., residual gas, thermal radiation), its wave function becomes entangled with the environmental degrees of freedom. For a fullerene passing through an interferometer, the wave function $\Psi_{total}(q, E, t)$ becomes a superposition of terms, each representing the fullerene having passed through a specific path (e.g., slit 1 or slit 2) and the environment being in a corresponding state $E_1$ or $E_2$.\n\n2.  **Impact on Particle Trajectories:** While the wave function $\Psi_{total}$ spreads and entangles, the particle itself, $Q(t)$, always follows a single, definite trajectory through one of the slits, guided by the guidance equation: $dQ_k/dt = (\hbar/m_k) * Im( (\nabla_k \Psi(q,t)) / \Psi(q,t) ) |_{q=Q(t)}$. However, because the guiding wave $\Psi$ has become entangled with the environment, the effective wave function guiding the particle's path becomes localized. The interference pattern, which arises from the superposition of paths, is statistically washed out because the environment effectively 'labels' which path the particle took, even though the particle itself always took a definite path.\n\n3.  **Statistical Equivalence:** The loss of fringe visibility is a statistical observable. Since dBBT's statistical predictions are identical to SQM's under quantum equilibrium, the quantitative prediction for the fringe visibility in this decoherence experiment will be the same as that derived from standard SQM.
